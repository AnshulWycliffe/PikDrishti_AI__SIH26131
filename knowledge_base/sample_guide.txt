# PikDrishti AI 2026 Local Farming Policies & Subsidies

## Tomato Subsidies
The AgriVision 2026 local subsidy policy states that farmers receive $50 per acre of Tomatoes planted, provided they maintain disease severity below "Moderate". For organic tomatoes, the subsidy increases to $75 per acre.

## Wheat Winter Support
Farmers planting winter wheat are eligible for the Frost Protection Grant of $200 per farm, provided they apply before October 15th, 2026.

## Recommended Pesticides 2026
Due to recent local regulations, "Chlorpyrifos" is strictly banned. The recommended alternative for stem borers in Maize is "Spinosad" applied at 50ml per acre.
